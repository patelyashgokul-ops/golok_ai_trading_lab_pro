# P08 scheduler and recovery

Odoo's two-minute action reads an explicit fixture calendar in Asia/Kolkata and rotates up to 32 watches per cycle. It distinguishes pre-market, opening, intraday, post-market, closed, maintenance and missing calendar. A transaction locks each watch and persists one report per date and phase with the observation count, NO TRADE summary and optional research run. Administrators can pause the watch or check its current phase. The operations list displays persisted records.

Intraday and post-market create descriptive snapshots only from observations already available at the cutoff. P08.1 uses the last persisted check to recover post-market reports for explicit completed sessions up to seven calendar days back. Recovery preserves the original session-close cutoff and labels results historical. Existing reports are reused and missed intraday phases are never manufactured. The first check starts on the current calendar date. Scheduled real acquisition and full daily risk reporting remain open.

The Odoo owner-control regression checks the manual phase action, denies access to an analyst and verifies pause and resume. It also confirms that an explicitly split exceptional session reports maintenance without a research result.

The native cron now commits a five-minute database claim before starting work. Expired claims can be recovered after worker exit. Each claim has a distinct token, and a worker with an expired or replaced token cannot publish. Attempts are capped at three. Publishing a research result and marking the job complete share a transaction. Request owners can retry eligible failed jobs or cancel queued jobs. Repeated requests preserve the same job and state. Authorization-blocked jobs require an effective grant and are not automatically retried.

The process test holds a claim in an independent process, skips it from an Odoo worker, terminates the claimant and checks for one recovered result. The committed-lease test advances the test clock, claims a lease once after expiry, rejects the old token and verifies a single result. The production lease duration was not shortened for the test.
