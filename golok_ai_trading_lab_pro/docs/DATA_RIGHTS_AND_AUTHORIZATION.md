# Data rights review — 24 November 2026

P07 adds an effective company review to generic real-market CSV import, in addition to Bhavcopy and SmartAPI. Query, display and archival rights must all be explicit. An expired or superseded grant blocks new imports. Historical purge and application-wide read and export controls remain open. The final paragraph below describes the P06 baseline. [P07 provider review](FREE_MARKET_DATA_PROVIDER_EVALUATION.md)

The native **Source Authorizations** form records the provider, documentation, terms, evidence, declared use, restrictions, expiry, storage policy and nine separate permissions. Odoo records the reviewer and creation time. New reviews supersede previous ones within the active company. An expired review or denied permission stops the import before its content is decoded. Archive storage requires explicit ongoing internal archival rights. Fixed-term retention and automatic purging remain open.

For NSE, public availability and the published MCP connection instructions establish a technical access route. The official terms restrict reuse and storage, and the market-data policy ties permitted uses to applicable agreements. The reviewed material did not establish permission for this project's automated acquisition, optimization or redistribution. The official Bhavcopy library lists the available reports. The importer accepts owner-authorized local files under a source review.
