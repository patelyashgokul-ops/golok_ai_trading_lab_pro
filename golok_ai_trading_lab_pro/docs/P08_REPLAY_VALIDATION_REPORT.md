# P08 replay validation

Both P07 synthetic policies reproduced their archived outputs. Market structure rejected every candidate. The explicit fixed 4R benchmark closed three simulated losing trades with gross −₹180, fees ₹6.06 and net −₹186.06. These are synthetic software outcomes.

The publication contract distinguishes daily descriptive research and intraday simulation. The intraday path verifies a fingerprint of the data and calendar, explicit permission, mapping and calendar attestations, quality evidence and a cost schedule. It processes publication as a separate event. Execution must occur strictly after publication. Contract tests cover delayed availability, missing rights, altered input, receipt-only timing and default synthetic-route rejection. Observations published after session close and out-of-order publications are rejected.

The gate evaluator accepts only literal affirmative evidence for a tuple result and reserves the verdict fields against replacement by metadata. The final decision explicitly blocks live actionability. The replay source inputs changed in this revision. Previously frozen, unconsumed holdouts with different implementation hashes fail closed. The Odoo portfolio wizard runs **Test Historical Timing on Generated Fixtures**. It takes server-generated, immutable synthetic datasets with fixture calendars, applies a one-minute modeled publication delay capped at each session close, and records the publication model, source digest and replay fingerprint. The holdout check applies before reading observations. The result tests Odoo integration and ledger timing with hypothetical tariffs.

**Run Authorized Historical Replay** uses a separately labeled Odoo result. It requires an active publisher-bound manual grant with backtest permission, reviewed unadjusted OHLCV in shares, a reviewed exchange calendar, an effective instrument mapping with ISIN and an explicit intraday tariff. A source administrator attests publication provenance by referencing reviewed HTTPS evidence and matching the complete stored dataset to the submitted CSV. Odoo records the reviewer and seals the dataset fingerprint. The attestation records the administrator's verification of the publisher's evidence. Odoo validates the matching data and its timestamps locally. The authorized route recomputes the full digest and rejects results after rights are withdrawn. Its tests used authored contract data and a test permission record. A genuine licensed file and publication evidence are still needed for a real-data validation.

The P07 strategy versions, ranking, risk controls and single-use synthetic holdout are preserved. Research results and supplemental findings overlapping the interval are hidden through user routes. Sealing rejects a date window already present in a prior research snapshot and serializes competing research and replay. Privileged database and direct filesystem access remain outside Odoo's trust boundary. Live actionability stays blocked.
