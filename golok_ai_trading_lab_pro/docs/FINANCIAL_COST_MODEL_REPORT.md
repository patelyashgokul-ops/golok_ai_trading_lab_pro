# P07 financial cost model

## P09 documented NSE cash equity reference

Reviewed 24 November 2026. NSE circular FA/73061 applies from 1 March 2026 and specifies ₹306.99 transaction charge plus ₹0.01 IPFT contribution per crore on each side, for combined member outflow of ₹307. NSE documents SEBI turnover fees, the buy-side 0.003% non-delivery stamp rate, GST and STT. Zerodha's published equity intraday schedule gives an example broker cap of the lower of 0.03% and ₹20 per executed order and a GST base of brokerage, exchange transaction charges and SEBI charges. The example does not establish a tariff for the owner's brokers. [NSE transaction charges](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA73061.pdf) · [NSE statutory levies](https://www.nseindia.com/static/invest/first-time-investor-sebi-turnover-fees-stt-other-levies) · [Zerodha charges](https://www.zerodha.com/charges)

On authored 1,000-share legs at ₹100 and ₹105, the existing Decimal engine reconciles ₹30.34 on entry, ₹53.78 on exit, ₹84.12 in estimated charges and ₹4,915.88 net. The case intentionally uses per-fill paise rounding and zero spread or slippage to make components independently checkable. The published STT method rounds the applicable total to whole rupees, so the estimate cannot serve as a contract-note verifier. Broker-level aggregation, account-specific brokerage, square-off fees and effective revisions require separate review. [STT rounding](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/how-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt-calculated) · [Reference test](../tests/unit_p09.py)

The effective-dated Decimal tariff engine remains hypothetical. Synthetic replay requires buy and sell tariff coverage and checks risk and reward after fees. Minimum accepted planned net reward divided by stressed net loss remains 2:1. Candidates reaching 3:1 receive ranking preference. The structure policy evaluates the nearest defensible prior level even if it fails the minimum. The benchmark can use fixed 4R when selected explicitly.

The prior independent cash and fee identities and conservative stop-first cases ran again. Verified Indian equity statutory charges, venue charges and broker-specific execution costs have yet to be reconciled against an authorized account or tariff.
