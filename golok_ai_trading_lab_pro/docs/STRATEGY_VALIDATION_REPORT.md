# Strategy validation — P06

The experimental portfolio registry now contains ST-01, ST-02, ST-04, ST-06 and ST-08. ST-04 requires a fast average above a slower average, a pullback, and a close above the preceding high. ST-06 requires a close above the preceding ten highs with at least twice the preceding mean volume. Both use the shared portfolio stop, cost, liquidity and risk checks. They run on information available by the synthetic bar close.

**Compare Five Strategies** creates five independently reproducible replays with identical dates, universe, capital, tick and tariff. **Walk-forward Validation** evaluates each strategy on two training dates, skips one embargo date and evaluates the best training net result among strategies with trades on the following date. A stable code ordering breaks ties. Each fold starts at the frozen input capital. A fold without a training trade yields no selection. Full fold-lineage reporting and statistical inference remain open.

**Seal Final Holdout** requires an earlier matching single-strategy portfolio and a previously unused date range after an embargo. Sealing stores a hash of the actual data, strategy, policy, fees and code before computing outcomes. Ordinary portfolio and legacy sequential replay reject reserved dates. A risk manager consumes the seal once. Database serialization protects competing requests and the result and consumption audit commit together. This controls application replay routes. Administrators, raw data readers and out-of-application analysis remain outside this isolation.

Three synthetic instruments on one session yielded three ST-01 losses, gross ₹−180.00 and net ₹−186.06. Doubling tariff rates produced net ₹−192.06. The other four strategies generated zero trades on this fixture. Separate constructed observations exercise the two new signal definitions. These data do not support a winner, calibration, a probability estimate or external financial validation. [Reproducible results](evidence/p06/strategy-competition.json)
