# P09 financial metrics and validation

**Calculate Performance** freezes a versioned, company-scoped metric report derived from the existing synthetic portfolio. It reconciles gross P&L, fees, net cash and completed-trade outcomes. Expectancy includes zero-net trades and deducts costs once. Zero trades return insufficient evidence. Closed-trade drawdown uses realized trade cash. End-of-day drawdown uses flat end-of-session equity. Intraday mark-to-market drawdown, capital exposure, MAE, MFE, Sharpe, Sortino and expectancy intervals remain unavailable on the current inputs.

The experimental walk-forward action freezes rolling or expanding folds over common regular trading sessions, with embargo periods before validation and evaluation. It records all five training trials and their immutable portfolio fingerprints. A positive net expectancy and at least two completed trades are required for selection. Drawdown, sample count and fee burden break ranking ties. Only the selected candidate is validated and evaluated. A failed validation is recorded without evaluation. The selector never reads evaluation outcomes. The existing application-level final holdout remains isolated from these experiments. Independent market regimes and optimized strategy parameters remain open. There are zero independently validated strategies on genuine market data.
