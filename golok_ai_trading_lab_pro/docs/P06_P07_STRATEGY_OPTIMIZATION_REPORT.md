# P06–P07 strategy optimization

- RE-21 proposes tick-aligned signal, prior breakout and approximate VWAP limits using completed observations. **Experimental causal entry, stop and target** applies the evaluator to the five historical portfolio strategies. It freezes the proposal at signal time and checks the later open against its range, limit and expiry.
- RE-22 compares strategy invalidation, recent swing, ATR and volatility-buffer stops. The Decimal sizing service checks gap stress, hypothetical costs, liquidity, capital, tick size and the two-to-one minimum net reward-to-risk. The replay records the selected stop and simulated fill. Stops stay fixed through the simulated position.
- RE-23 adds **Experimental ATR-capped resistance** to historical portfolio replay. It uses the nearer completed resistance or two ATR above the entry, whichever is lower, with tick alignment in the replay. Insufficient volatility history or a missing defensible barrier yields no target. Published structure and fixed 4R benchmark policies retained their definitions and archived synthetic results.
- With the joint policy selected, **Run Walk-forward Experiment** evaluates 20 strategy–policy pairs per training fold. The same frozen strategy and policy carry through validation and, if eligible, evaluation. The report keeps zero-trade and losing trials. Wider parameter calibration, historical excursion statistics and genuine performance remain open.
- A prospective fixture account can compare all five strategies across its due instruments. Jobs sharing an exact cutoff compete after the cohort is ready and before the shortest candle interval elapses. The same cost-aware rank prefers plans above 3:1, then considers net ratio, planned reward and liquidity. One reservation uses the account risk controls. The selected and rejected candidates retain their strategy trials, cutoff, deadline and allocation evidence.
- **Fit a target cap on training observations** adds a training-only calibrated ATR variant. It samples completed interior closes and the modeled exit of the training benchmark trades. The median favorable fractional move is capped at 10% and requires trades from two separate sessions. A fitted cap can only lower the existing ATR and resistance ceiling. A viable cap is scored on training dates and any selected value stays fixed through validation and evaluation. Samples with insufficient evidence are reported explicitly.
