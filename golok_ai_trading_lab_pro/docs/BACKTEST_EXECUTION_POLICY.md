# ST-01 synthetic backtest, version 1

Run `python -B scripts/run_backtest.py SPEC.json CANDLES.csv` from the module directory. Use the temporal CSV format and the example under `tests/fixtures/p02`. The JSON defines the session, venue, tick size, data label, declared partition and explicit tariffs. The result writes a deterministic specification, input digest, experiment identifier, recorded candidates and any single simulated trade to standard output.

The replay accepts one complete, regular, explicitly synthetic session. It freezes the 15-minute opening high and low after the required observations are available. A later first crossing of the range high is checked on close and compared with 1.5× the previous three volumes. The range width must be within 4% of its high. No entry can occur at an opening that coincides with or precedes the decision’s availability. A delayed candidate expires if the later opening exceeds one interval, the configured entry cutoff, the opening range or the 1% entry deviation allowance.

Stop is the opening range low. Target is four times entry-to-stop distance above entry, subject to the independent fee-aware risk veto. The replay permits at most one fill per session. It closes for stop, conservative target, exit cutoff, or after 12 bars. An opening at or below stop exits at the opening print. The stop wins a same-candle ambiguity. A target requires trade-through and fills at the target without favorable gap improvement. Forced exits use observed closing marks with modeled friction, so market execution feasibility remains an assumption.

The simulation contract uses gross observed prices with separate spread/slippage tariffs. Tariffs are effective on `starts <= date < ends`, grouped by side, venue and segment and rounded independently to paise. Each side must explicitly name material charge categories, including any assumed zero. GST uses brokerage + exchange + SEBI under this modeled policy. Examples use hypothetical tariffs. The risk engine limits shares by capital after estimated entry fees, stressed stop losses including round-trip fees, and prior observed liquidity. It vetoes net reward below twice the projected loss. Planned loss is an estimate and adverse gaps may exceed it. No current tariff pack is supplied. [Angel One’s published charge schedule](https://www.angelone.in/exchange-transaction-charges)

Partition names are declared metadata and do not themselves enforce holdout isolation. A real historical experiment requires authorized point-in-time source data, effective-dated reference data, audited corporate actions, declared portfolio and validation protocols, and a verified tariff pack. No strategy can issue actionable real-market recommendations. No paper account is created by replay.
